Classtago R2.5.36 — PARENT SIGNUP + PEON OPERATIONS + BELL ALARM
=================================================================
Base: R2.5.35 cumulative project.

Parent signup visibility
- Parent Sign Up is now visibly rendered on the main Login screen alongside Staff Sign Up and Student Sign Up.
- Existing R2.5.35 Parent verification, Headmaster approval and child-link security flow is reused; no duplicate Parent backend was created.

Peon role
- Additive UserRole: peon.
- Peon is created through the existing Staff Master / Staff Sign Up chain; Peon/Attendant/Helper/Support Staff designation is inferred as the Peon role.
- Headmaster retains account activation/deactivation authority.
- Peon portal is least-privilege: Duty Dashboard, Bell Timing & Alarm, Gate & Visitor Desk, Office Dispatch & Delivery, My Leave, Notices & Instructions, Profile & Security.
- Peon does not receive Admission, Academic Setup, Teacher Assignment, Marks/Result editing, Fees/Accounts, Payroll or account-approval authority.
- Peon profile uses the existing Complete Profile engine as staff and uses Employee Code / username as protected login identity.

Bell Timing & Alarm
- Headmaster-only Bell Timing & Alarm Control supports two independent sources:
  1) Timetable mode: read-only school period timings from active Academic Setup.
  2) Manual mode: Headmaster-defined bell schedule with effective dates, days and custom events.
- Manual Bell Schedule does not modify the academic timetable.
- Peon has no alarm OFF switch and cannot edit Bell Timing.
- Native Android BellAlarm Capacitor plugin uses exact local Android alarms, persists the schedule, and reschedules after reboot/app update/time/timezone change.
- Bell sound uses Android alarm audio usage. Explicit phone Silent mode suppresses sound; other OS-level notification/DND policy remains controlled by Android.
- Exact-alarm permission/readiness is surfaced in Peon Bell Timing.

Leave-aware bell safety
- Bell schedule generation is scoped to the currently authenticated Peon user.
- Approved Peon leave ranges are read from the canonical Leave Management audit workflow.
- Full-day approved leave removes all bell alarms for the leave date(s).
- Half-day approved leave removes only bells in that leave session, using the active day's bell span to determine the session boundary.
- Future approved leave dates are removed before the native schedule is sent to the Peon device.
- When the Peon app refreshes/returns to foreground, replaceSchedule cancels previously stored alarms that are no longer authorized by the current leave-aware schedule.
- Peon Portal refreshes leave/bell state on foreground and every five minutes while visible.
- After leave ends, subsequent valid bells remain in the authoritative schedule automatically.

Preservation
- No Supabase schema/migration file changed.
- No existing file was removed.
- Existing Headmaster, Clerk, Teacher, Student, Parent, Admission, Result, Timetable and Communication engines were not replaced.
